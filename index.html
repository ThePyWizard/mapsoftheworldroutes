What is this?

Every viral roadtrip, on one spinning planet.

I post travel itineraries on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube as @mapsoftheworldroutes. Each one is animated using TravelAnimator. This site brings them all together — pick a route, preview it on the globe, and open the exact same animation in the app.

How it works
  1. 1 Spin the globe and click any glowing arc.
  2. 2 Read the script and see what makes the route trending.
  3. 3 Tap Open in TravelAnimator — the app launches the exact route.
